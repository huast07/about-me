Volunteering.

City of Toronto
Cultural Events

In 2023, I first began volunteering with the City of Toronto Cultural Events. In 2026, I became the youngest Volunteer Captain and have led teams of over 15 volunteers, whose backgrounds range from students to new immigrants to Canada.

Past events I have volunteered for include the Taylor Swift Eras Tour, Doors Open Toronto, New Year’s Eve, Canada Day, and Nuit Blanche.

FIFA World Cup

For the 2026 FIFA World Cup, I was lucky enough to be selected from around 248,000 applicants. I volunteered as a Host City Ambassador, welcoming and directing football fans from across the world.

Here are some of my favourite memories.

Land

Wind Whistle Farm

During my fall reading week, I did a workstay through WWOOF at Wind Whistle Farm in Honeywood, Ontario. I lived in a tent on the property and worked alongside several other “WWOOFers” from across the world.
