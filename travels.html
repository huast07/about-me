Travel takeaways

If there’s one thing my travels have taught me, it’s an appreciation for the vast and remarkably different world we call home.

From the bustling metropolises of North America to the rainforest-enveloped villages of Cambodia, people lead vastly different lives, face different struggles, and see the world through different lenses. Yet, somehow, we find happiness in remarkably similar things: love, family, community, kindness, and good conversation.

But, perhaps the greatest realization lies in how, despite the borders, ideologies, and cultures that divide us, we are far more alike than we are different. We need look beyond the systems and beliefs that shape our lives to question how and why they took shape, but also seek to find and appreciate that humanity we all share.

The world is far too vast for any one perspective to define it, and the more I see of it, the more I realize how little I truly know.
Caspar David Friedrich · Wanderer above the Sea of Fog (c. 1818) · Public domain.
